# astop

astop displays telemetry for the Ascend NPUs on the reference machine.

## Language

**Device card**:
The terminal display for one NPU, including its identity, telemetry and utilization meters.

**Link sample**:
A reading of PCIe or HCCS traffic and availability for one NPU. An unavailable reading is distinct from a measured zero.

**HCCS**:
The Ascend interconnect between NPUs. Traffic rates describe the configured links together.

**HBM**:
The high-bandwidth memory attached to an NPU.
